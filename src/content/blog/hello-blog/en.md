description: "Seven years later, I finally have a blog of my own 🤪"
date: "Feb 6 2026"
---

I started working in 2018, and ever since then I had this recurring thought: one day I should really build myself a personal blog.

But for all kinds of reasons, I kept putting it off:

- Every time I wanted to write something, I'd wonder if the post was too lame or if nobody would care.
- The blog themes I found never felt quite right, and I definitely did not feel like designing and building one from scratch.
- I kept overthinking and going in circles, spending all my energy on the least important parts.

Looking back, it's kind of funny. I was worrying way too much about things that had not even happened yet.

Lately though, I've been trying to change that mindset a bit. A blog is really just for recording and sharing things. That's the whole point. Everything else is extra.

So in the end, I spent one day building this site, and now... here it is. My blog is finally real.

The road here was a little twisty, sure, but hey, we made it. Happy start!
